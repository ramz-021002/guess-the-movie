How to play

  1. Read the line out loud. Everyone shouts which movie, series or special it comes from.
  2. Stuck? Tap Hint to see the moment: what's happening in the scene.
  3. Tap Reveal when you're ready, then Next line for another.
  4. Above the card, pick a difficulty and whether you want Movies, TV shows or Both.
